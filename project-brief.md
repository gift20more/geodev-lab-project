                  Project Topic
GIS-Based Assessment of Water Availability and Accessibility in Malawi
                  Area of Interest
My area of interest is Water Resources Engineering, with a focus on
Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH), GIS, Remote Sensing and
environmental management.

                   Project Problem
Some communities may have limited access to safe and reliable water
sources. GIS can be used to identify the location of water sources and
analyse their accessibility to communities.

          Questions the Project Will Answer
1. Where are water sources located?
2. Which areas have good or poor access to water sources?
3. How far are communities from available water sources?
4. Which areas may require additional water points?
5. How can GIS support water resource planning and management?

             Proposed Solution

The project will use GIS to map water sources and communities and
analyse their spatial accessibility. The results can help identify
areas that may require improved water supply services and support
better planning of water infrastructure.

             Datasets Required

The project will require:

1. Water point locations
2. Population data
3. Settlement data
4. Administrative boundaries
5. Road network data
6. Land use and land cover data
7. Satellite imagery

          Dataset Sources

The datasets will be obtained from reliable open-data sources,
including:

- Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX)
- OpenStreetMap
- WorldPop
- USGS EarthExplorer
- Google Earth Engine

           Expected Outcome
The project is expected to produce maps and spatial analysis showing
the distribution and accessibility of water sources. The results
will help identify areas where water access may need improvement
and support better water-resource planning and management.
